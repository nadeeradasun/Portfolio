About

Professional summary

Cybersecurity Analyst with over four years' experience supporting Security Operations Centre (SOC) monitoring, incident triage and threat detection across enterprise environments for Australian and international customers. Skilled in analysing security telemetry across SIEM (ELK Stack), Endpoint Detection & Response (EDR) and Intrusion Detection (IDS) platforms, with hands-on exposure to firewall and network security tools (PFsense, SonicWall) across Linux and Windows.

Experienced in vulnerability assessment (OpenVAS, Acunetix, Burp Suite, OWASP ZAP), malware and phishing investigation, root-cause analysis, and documenting remediation and operating procedures. Holds a Certificate IV in Cyber Security (Victoria University) and a Microsoft Cybersecurity Analyst Professional Certificate, underpinned by an engineering background and continuous professional development including AI prompt-injection and DLP security testing.
